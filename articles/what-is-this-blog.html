What Is This Blog?

This blog is a place for me to materialize some of the thoughts I have about finance and investing.

I am a Canadian fund manager with industry-agnostic responsibility for public equities and nearly a decade of investing experience.

Although I am classically trained and I have worked on various "streets" across the world in front-office high finance, I find that the majority of my investing success stems directly from knowledge I've accrued outside those settings. Traditional finance attracts some very smart people, but most of them are not good investors (even on the buy-side).

If you're looking for systematic coverage of specific finance topics, this is not the blog for you. However, if you have an open mind and are willing to think deeply about the common financial wisdom floating in the world with a critical eye, then I welcome you.

I hope you enjoy your reading.
